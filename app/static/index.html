YOLOv8 Detection API

Pretrained YOLOv8n (COCO, 80 classes) exported to ONNX and served by FastAPI on a CPU. Pre- and post-processing (letterbox, decode, NMS) and the IoU tracker are plain NumPy. Endpoints: /docs · /health · /metrics · GitHub

Drop an image or a short clip, or use a sample.
Raw JSON response

  
Image results show preprocess, inference and postprocess time. A clip is queued on /jobs (one at a time, 10 seconds max) and comes back as an annotated MP4. Webcam mode posts frames to /detect. Self-hosted servers also expose /ws/detect.
